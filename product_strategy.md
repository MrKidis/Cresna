# Product Strategy: A Growth-Intelligence Loop for Shopify Brands

Cresna will **not** position itself as a generic “AI assistant for Shopify,” a standalone profit tracker, or an SEO content generator. Those categories already have mature products. For example, TrueProfit markets real-time profit, cost, product, and attribution tracking, while Bloom combines contribution margin, costs, attribution, and AI analysis. Shopify SEO tools also already package product SEO, image optimization, metadata, schema, and AI-search optimization in a single suite. [1] [2] [3]

The initial product wedge is an **evidence-bound growth-intelligence loop for small and midsize Shopify brands**: connect a store, set the business goals, build a persistent Growth Profile, surface the highest-leverage opportunity, let the merchant approve a change, and measure the result. The product must never create a score, result, competitor claim, traffic signal, or financial outcome when it lacks the underlying data. Instead, it should state what it scanned, what evidence it has, and what extra connection or permission would be needed for a broader finding.

| Product layer | Initial promise | Evidence required | Explicit boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Growth Profile** | Remember the merchant’s goals, products, positioning, and prior actions. | Merchant-provided goals and approved Shopify data. | Does not infer brand strategy or a target audience without merchant input. |
| **Opportunity Engine** | Rank a short list of concrete sales, refund, product, and checkout opportunities. | Store aggregates, products, refunds, checkout activity, and approved scans. | Does not pretend generic SEO hints are unique competitive intelligence. |
| **Approval Layer** | Explain *what*, *why*, *impact*, *effort*, and the evidence before an action. | Source-linked recommendation inputs and an estimated—not guaranteed—impact range. | No blind editing or store automation. |
| **Outcome Memory** | Record accepted and rejected recommendations, then compare transparent pre- and post-action periods. | A sufficient baseline and a completed comparison window. | Does not claim causality when the data only shows correlation. |

The first launch should serve **owner-led stores with roughly one to a few operators**, beginning with product and revenue signals already accessible under the current read scopes. The product will support broader content, competitor, search, social, and AI-visibility intelligence only after it has the relevant consented source data and a precise, testable scan method. This is deliberately narrower than “make any store popular,” but it is a credible path to becoming the merchant’s operating system for growth decisions.

The pricing model is intentionally simple: **Pro at $29/month** for smaller teams and **Growth at $79/month** for businesses that need more frequent intelligence and deeper capabilities, both with a **14-day full-access trial**. Founding Beta access will be invitation-only, lasts seven days, and is designed to collect structured feedback rather than artificial testimonials.
