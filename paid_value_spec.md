# Cresna Paid-Value Product Specification

## Product promise

> **Cresna is your store’s AI growth partner: connect your store, discover what is holding it back, get an evidence-backed plan, and approve the work that improves it.**

The paid product is not an AI chat box, an SEO score, or a generic copy generator. The defensible loop is **Scan → Recommend → Generate → Approve → Measure**. Cresna earns a subscription only when it translates verified merchant data into a prioritized, approved action and keeps the decision history needed to learn whether that action helped.

| Differentiator | Cresna commitment | Explicit boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opportunity Engine** | Ranks specific business opportunities with source evidence, effort, confidence, and estimated impact. | It does not invent competitors, search rankings, shopper behavior, or revenue outcomes. |
| **Business Brain** | Preserves merchant-provided positioning, goals, brand voice, approved changes, rejections, and observed outcome windows. | It does not claim permanent memory outside the saved merchant profile and decision records. |
| **AI actions** | Generates reviewable work products that inherit approved brand context, beginning with product-description and positioning drafts. | It never writes back to Shopify until the merchant reviews and expressly approves a future publish action. |
| **Growth Score** | Explains its components and data coverage, then degrades to an insufficient-data state when inputs are unavailable. | It is not a fabricated universal benchmark or a promise of sales. |

## Why the product must be more than free AI

Shopify describes Sidekick as a built-in assistant that can generate content, help with store workflows, and work from merchant data; it is included in Shopify plans.[1] Cresna should therefore avoid competing on one-off writing prompts. Its paid value is a persistent, cross-workflow intelligence layer: a merchant’s goals and position inform every scan; every recommendation is evidence-bound; every proposed change requires merchant approval; and completed work can be measured against a transparent baseline.

Shopify’s own Sidekick extension guidance reinforces this action pattern: app data should be factual and read-only, while mutations belong in scoped actions where the merchant confirms the update.[2] Cresna will follow the same trust model.

## Release order for the paid core

| Priority | Capability | First real version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Business Brain** | Store goals, brand context, target buyer, voice, differentiators, product and decision history. |
| **2** | **Store Scan** | Shopify products, orders, refunds, discounts, price and cost signals, product metadata, product-description completeness, collections, and merchant-provided context. |
| **3** | **Growth Score** | Revenue health, catalog quality, conversion evidence, offer quality, and brand-context coverage; each component shows source coverage. |
| **4** | **Opportunity Engine** | One ranked recommendation at a time with what, why, evidence, estimated impact range, confidence, effort, and an approval gate. |
| **5** | **AI Action Studio** | Generate product-description and positioning drafts that preserve approved voice; compare before and after; select individual products; approve drafts. |
| **6** | **Outcome Learning** | Store decision, execution date, baseline, comparison period, measured result, and merchant feedback. |

## Worth-paying-for plan design

The launch plans remain intentionally simple. Both include a **14-day full-access trial** and no fabricated capability claims.

| Plan | Price | Paid reason to choose it | Initial limits |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Cresna Pro** | **$19/month** | A solo operator receives the full Scan → Recommend → Generate → Approve → Measure loop for one connected store. | One store; standard scan cadence; capped AI action generations. |
| **Cresna Growth** | **$49/month** | A growing team receives deeper catalog review, more frequent scans, more AI action generations, and priority-ranked opportunities. | Multiple stores only after verified multi-store support is implemented; no unbuilt competitor or AI-search claims. |
| **Owner / Founder Mode** | **No charge** | Unrestricted access for the designated owner account, including Founder Mode and controlled beta flags. | Restricted to the owner role, not a public plan. |

## AI visibility and competitor intelligence

AI-search visibility is strategically relevant, but it cannot be a decorative score. Shopify’s current guidance emphasizes factual product-page detail, traffic and conversion baselines, and tracking whether AI referrals lead to revenue.[3] Cresna will first identify **product citability and positioning gaps** from the merchant’s own catalog. Prompt tracking, source monitoring, and verified competitor comparisons are later capabilities, enabled only when Cresna has a reliable data source and can disclose the observation method.
